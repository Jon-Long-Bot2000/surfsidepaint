    A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    Do I need a subscription?+

    Yes. Customers need a subscription to request handyman work or create a tailored yearly maintenance plan. Creating an account or saving a To-Do does not enroll you in the subscription or book a visit.

    Can Surfside see my saved To-Dos?+

    Your saved To-Dos and photos stay private to your household until you explicitly review and submit the items you want help with. Only your selected items are shared with Surfside.

    What does the $150 monthly subscription include?+

    The subscription includes two hours of handyman service each month, with subscription payments applied toward service invoices. You also have tailored yearly maintenance planning and service coordination. Materials and fixtures are charged separately.

    Is the Home Health Inspection included?+

    No. The Home Health Inspection is a recommended To-Do that subscribers can find in Discover. It is not included in the monthly subscription.

    Does requesting service confirm an appointment?+

    No. Choose the To-Dos you want help with and review your service request before sending it. Surfside follows up to coordinate your visit; an appointment is confirmed separately.

    Do I need to download an app?+

    No download is required. Use the Client Portal in your browser. You sign up with phone verification, with no email required at registration.
