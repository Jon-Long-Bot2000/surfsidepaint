Water-activated sculpture Motion study 01
Wooden slats form a narrow column at rest. Water pours into the central bowl, the slats spread outward, hold open, and return to rest.

A simplified model based on Jon's sculpture prototype: a ring of pale wooden slats, cords, a suspended bowl, and a metal stand. The cycle moves from resting to water input, activated, open, and back to resting. Use the stage buttons to pause at a particular state.
