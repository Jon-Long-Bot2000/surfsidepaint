Homes color reference

The first nine swatches are the core palette. The remaining colors cover map details, interface accents, illustration details, and transparent shadows. This inventory includes color declarations retained in the current stylesheet and SVG definitions, including styles whose elements have since been removed. Raster photo colors and the six UI studies are excluded.
