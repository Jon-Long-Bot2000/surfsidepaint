About this sample

Estimates assume a single-story, 2,500 sq. ft. residence. They illustrate hands-on service time, rather than a quote or guaranteed completion time. Timing and scope would be tailored to the home, access, condition, and weather; materials are separate.

The full selection schedules two estimated service hours in every month: 24 hours across the year. The annual meter and monthly coverage are separate, so a full-year total never hides a gap in a particular month. The two-hour monthly target is used for this study; unused subscription hours can roll over up to six months.

No account or service request is created. These are local design studies; the Homes page is unchanged.
